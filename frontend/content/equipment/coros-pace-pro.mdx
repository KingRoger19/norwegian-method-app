I switched to COROS from Garmin after reading too many posts about battery life and GPS accuracy on long runs. Six months later, here's where I stand.

## What Works Well

**Battery life** is genuinely exceptional. I can run 4–5 hours with GPS and heart rate on and still have charge left. On long training weeks this matters — I don't want to think about charging.

**Heart rate accuracy** is solid for steady-state efforts. During threshold sessions where pace and HR are fairly stable, the wrist sensor tracks well against chest strap comparisons.

**The app and API** export your data in a usable format. I built my own analytics dashboard on top of it — if COROS didn't let me get my data out, I wouldn't have kept it.

## Where It Falls Short

HR accuracy during intensity spikes is noticeably behind a chest strap. For VO₂max intervals where HR is climbing fast, there's lag. Not a dealbreaker for Norwegian Method training (where most work is steady threshold), but worth knowing.

The native app is functional but basic. I don't use it for analysis — that's what this site is for.

## Would I Buy It Again?

Yes. For the price, battery life, and data accessibility, it's the right tool for the training I do.
