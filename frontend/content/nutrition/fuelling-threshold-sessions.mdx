Double-threshold days are the cornerstone of the Norwegian Method. Two sessions, both in the LT1–LT2 zone, separated by 4–6 hours. Getting the fuel right matters more than most runners realise.

## Morning Session (06:00)

Before an early threshold run, I keep it light:

- 1 slice sourdough with peanut butter
- 1 banana
- 350ml water with a pinch of salt

The goal is topping up liver glycogen from overnight without overloading the stomach. High-fat or high-fibre food before a threshold run is asking for trouble.

## Recovery Window (08:30–12:30)

This window is the most important meal of the day. I have 4–5 hours to:

1. Replenish glycogen stores
2. Stimulate protein synthesis
3. Keep inflammation manageable

In practice: oats, eggs, Greek yogurt, and some fruit. Carbohydrates first, then protein. The exact quantities are on the [Nutrition page](/nutrition) of the app.

## Pre-Afternoon Session (16:30)

90 minutes before the PM session:

- 3 rice cakes with jam or honey
- 1 banana
- 500ml water

Simple carbs, minimal fat and fibre. Nothing that will slow gastric emptying.

## Evening Recovery (20:00)

After the second threshold session, I prioritise protein and complex carbs:

- Lean protein (150g beef or chicken)
- Sweet potatoes or pasta
- Vegetables with olive oil

Sleep quality after a double-threshold day is everything — the adaptation happens at night.
